# Privacy Policy: WaveRelief
**Last Updated: April 2026**

WaveRelief operates as a standalone, 100% offline acoustic engine.

### CHILDREN'S PRIVACY
WaveRelief does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

### DATA PROTECTION
* **No Data Collection:** This machine does not access, collect, or store any personal information.
* **No Transmission:** No data is ever transmitted to external servers.
* **Local Processing:** All signal generation occurs locally on your device.
